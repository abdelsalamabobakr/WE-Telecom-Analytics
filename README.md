# WE-Telecom-Analytics
Power BI analytics dashboard covering churn analysis, network performance, and revenue KPIs for 50K telecom customers

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop**: Data Modeling, Interactive Dashboard Creation, and Visualization.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Calculated Measures and Columns for KPIs (Churn Rate, Revenue, Downtime).
* **Power Query**: Data Cleaning, Transformation, and Structuring.

---

## 📊 Data Source
* **Telecom Customer Dataset**: Synthetic/Anonymized dataset containing 50K customer records covering churn indicators, revenue metrics, network performance, and service interactions.

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Findings
* **Network Downtime Impact**: Cities with the highest dropped call rates showed a direct correlation with customer churn, highlighting priority regions for infrastructure upgrades.
* **CSAT & Support Efficiency**: First Contact Resolution (FCR) rate heavily influences Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) scores—improving support response times reduces churn risk.
* **Revenue Drivers**: High-value plans generate the majority of profit margins, but targeted retention packages are needed for mid-tier plan subscribers prone to switching.
